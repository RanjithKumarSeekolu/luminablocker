Privacy Policy

LuminaBlocker (“Lumina”, “we”, “the app”)
Effective date: 18 September 2026

LuminaBlocker is a digital wellbeing app that helps you pause distracting apps and run focus sessions. This policy explains what data the app uses, what stays on your phone, and what may leave the device.

1. No account

Lumina does not require sign-in. We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or payment details inside the app.

2. Data stored on your device

The following stay on your phone unless you export them:

  • Apps you choose to block, and per-app settings (intensity, mindful reminder, night lock)
  • Usage and focus statistics (time of day, session history, scores, overlay events)
  • Your preferred focus duration and in-app toggles

This on-device data is used only to block apps, show overlays, run focus mode, and display your own insights. It is protected by your device lock (PIN, pattern, or biometrics).

3. Permissions

Lumina is a digital wellbeing tool. It uses three Android permissions:

  • Accessibility — to notice when an app you marked as distracting is opened, so a mindful pause can appear. Lumina does not read the content of your messages, emails, passwords, or what you type.
  • Display over other apps (overlay) — to show the pause screen and focus lock over other apps.
  • Usage access — to show your screen-time patterns for apps you track.

You can revoke any of these in Android Settings. Blocking and insights will stop working until they are granted again.

4. Data that may leave your device

If Growth Insights are enabled in your build, Lumina may send aggregate information to OpenRouter (openrouter.ai) so a language model can write a short usage summary. That payload can include:

  • Names and categories of apps you chose to track (for example “YouTube”, “VIDEO”)
  • Rounded usage durations and weekly totals
  • Intervention scores and levels

It does not include your identity, contacts, photos, message content, or a list of every app on the phone. OpenRouter and its model providers process that request under their own policies: https://openrouter.ai/privacy.

We do not sell your data. We do not use it for ads. We do not run a Lumina account database of your usage.

5. Export and deletion

In Settings → Data & Privacy Vault you can export JSON or CSV of your local history, or erase all local Lumina data. Erasing cannot remove a Growth Insight request that was already sent to OpenRouter.

6. Children

Lumina is intended for people 18 and over. It is not directed at children and is not part of Google Play’s Designed for Families program.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
